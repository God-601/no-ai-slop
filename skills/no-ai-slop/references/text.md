# Text review mode

Use this mode for prose, captions, scripts, emails, essays, descriptions, and other written content.

## Preserve first

- Keep the writer's intended point, factual meaning, audience, and level of formality.
- Retain distinctive wording when it works. Do not force slang, typos, confessions, or personal anecdotes to make the text seem more human.
- Do not add facts, examples, citations, opinions, or lived experience that the writer did not provide or authorize.

## Look for concrete quality problems

- Repeated claims, filler introductions, recaps, and conclusions that merely repeat what is already clear.
- Stock transitions or dramatic framing that announces importance without adding information.
- Parallel lists, emphatic contrasts, slogans, and rhetorical questions used by habit instead of purpose.
- Abstract claims where the text could name the actual person, object, action, example, or consequence.
- Overly even sentence rhythm, uniform paragraph length, excessive signposting, or a register that does not fit the audience.
- Unsupported superlatives, vague authority claims, invented certainty, or facts that need verification.
- Chat residue such as prefatory apologies, "here's a polished version" wrappers, or notes meant for the requester rather than the final reader.

These are review prompts, not banned forms. Keep any pattern that serves the argument or the writer's voice.

## Edit

1. State the point earlier when the draft delays it.
2. Replace vague wording with details already supported by the context.
3. Remove a repeated sentence rather than rewriting it with synonyms.
4. Vary sentence shapes only where it improves emphasis or flow; do not manufacture irregularity.
5. Match the intended register and language variety. Preserve the author's voice instead of imposing a default polished tone.
6. Flag claims that cannot be checked from available context; do not silently make them sound more certain.

For Brazilian Portuguese, also read [`text-pt-br.md`](text-pt-br.md). When the user explicitly asks to match their own voice or provides a writing sample for that purpose, read [`voice-matching.md`](voice-matching.md). Do not infer or save a personal voice profile without permission.

## Final pass

Confirm that the revised text still means the same thing, each paragraph adds something, and no new claim or personal detail slipped in. Provide the revised text first, then summarize the substantive edits.
