# Voice matching

Use this when the user asks to preserve or match their own voice and supplies a
writing sample or style guide. A sample is evidence about this task, not a
permanent profile.

## Workflow

1. Use only the sample or style guide the user supplied or explicitly approved.
2. Notice a few relevant traits: formality, sentence length, directness,
   vocabulary, contractions, punctuation, and use of humor or regional speech.
3. Keep those traits only where they fit the target audience and format.
4. Preserve the source's facts, intent, uncertainty, and constraints. Do not
   copy unrelated personal details from the sample into the new text.
5. If the sample is very short or conflicts with the requested context, match
   only clear traits and say what remains uncertain.
6. Return the requested text and briefly name the traits you preserved.

## Boundaries

- Do not add misspellings, slang, personal anecdotes, or unusual punctuation
  just to make the result appear human.
- Do not claim to know the user's personality or identity from a sample.
- Do not retain a sample, build a reusable profile, or apply it to later tasks
  unless the user asks for that.
- If the user provides no sample, preserve the voice evident in the current
  artifact. Do not invent a personal voice.
